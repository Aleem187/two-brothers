Our Story

Two Brothers.
One Passion.

What started as two brothers with a love for real, honest food has become Woodbridge's go-to spot for the best halal fried chicken in town.

We do things the hard way because it's the right way. Our chicken is antibiotic-free, hand-cut and marinated in-house every day. Our beef is grass-fed, hormone-free and ground fresh for every burger. Nothing frozen, nothing pre-made — just bold flavor and quality you can taste in every bite.

  • 100% Halal certified
  • Antibiotic-free chicken
  • Grass-fed, ground-fresh beef
  • Marinated & made in-house

Ask about our signature Moe sauce — the secret behind every unforgettable bite.
